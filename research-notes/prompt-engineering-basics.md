# Prompt Engineering Basics

## Current Understanding

Today I studied the fundamentals of prompt engineering and how prompts may be used with LLMs for the VIVA-AI project.

One important thing I learned is that the quality of AI output depends heavily on how prompts are structured.

Prompt engineering focuses on designing prompts that guide the AI toward better, more accurate, and more structured outputs.

---

# What is Prompt Engineering?

Current understanding:

Prompt engineering is the process of designing and refining prompts to improve AI responses.

The prompt acts as an instruction system for the AI model.

Better prompts may improve:
- output quality
- consistency
- structure
- relevance
- educational accuracy

---

# Important Prompt Engineering Concepts Learned

## Instruction Prompting

Instruction prompting means clearly telling the AI what task to perform.

Example:

Generate 3 multiple-choice questions based on the student submission.

Current understanding:
Clear instructions improve output quality.

---

## Role Prompting

Role prompting assigns a role or persona to the AI model.

Example:

You are an educational assessment assistant.

Current understanding:
Role prompting may improve:
- tone
- structure
- educational focus
- response consistency

---

## Zero-Shot Prompting

Zero-shot prompting means giving instructions without examples.

Example:

Generate 3 MCQs based on the student submission.

Current understanding:
The AI generates output without seeing sample examples first.

---

## One-Shot Prompting

One-shot prompting means giving one example before the actual task.

Current understanding:
Examples may help guide formatting and improve consistency.

---

## Few-Shot Prompting

Few-shot prompting means providing multiple examples.

Current understanding:
Few-shot prompting may improve:
- formatting consistency
- educational quality
- output structure
- reliability

---

# Prompt Priming

Prompt priming involves setting initial instructions that guide the AI's behavior throughout the conversation.

Example:

You are an educational assistant specialized in generating verification quizzes.

Current understanding:
Priming may help:
- maintain educational tone
- improve consistency
- guide response behavior

---

# Importance of Context

One major concept I learned is that AI performs better when enough context is provided.

Possible context may include:
- assignment requirements
- student submission
- rubric information
- formatting instructions

Without context, the AI may generate generic or inaccurate responses.
